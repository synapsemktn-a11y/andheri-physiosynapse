Cranio-Visceral Therapy

A gentle, hands-on approach that combines Craniosacral Therapy and Visceral Manipulation. Using light, precise touch, our therapists work on the tissues around the head, spine and internal organs to release tension the body has been holding — often where regular treatment hasn't helped.

Craniosacral Therapy

Very light touch around the skull, spine and pelvis to ease restrictions in the membranes and fluid that surround the brain and spinal cord, and to settle an over-stressed nervous system.

  • Chronic headache and migraine
  • Neck, jaw and back tension
  • Stress, poor sleep and anxiety
  • Supportive care for children with delayed milestones
Barral approach

Visceral Manipulation

Gentle, specific hand movements over the abdomen to improve the mobility of the organs and the connective tissue around them, which can affect digestion, posture and pain.

  • Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS) and other gut disorders
  • Bloating, constipation and abdominal discomfort
  • Back pain linked to the abdomen and pelvis
  • Tightness after abdominal surgery

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What is Cranio-Visceral Therapy?
It brings together two gentle manual therapies: Craniosacral Therapy, which works around the head, spine and pelvis, and Visceral Manipulation, which works on the internal organs and their surrounding tissue. Both use light, precise touch to release tension and restrictions in the body.
Is it painful?
No. The touch is very light and most people find it deeply relaxing. Tell your therapist at any time if something feels uncomfortable and they will adjust.
What should I wear?
Comfortable, loose clothing. Craniosacral and visceral work is done fully clothed. For physiotherapy of a specific joint, clothing that lets us see and move that area helps.
Can it be combined with regular physiotherapy?
Yes. Many plans at the clinic combine Cranio-Visceral work with manual therapy, exercise or neuro rehabilitation. Your therapist will recommend what suits your condition after the first assessment.
